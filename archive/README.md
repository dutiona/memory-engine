# Archive

Raw research and review artifacts from the memory-engine design process.
These have been superseded by the official documentation in `docs/`.

## Contents

### debates/

Multi-AI adversarial debates (3 rounds: Claude, Codex, Gemini) that informed
the architecture. Key decisions are now captured in [ADRs](../docs/design/adr/).

### reviews/

Plan review outputs from Codex and Gemini during the documentation planning
process.

### research-journal.md

Chronological research narrative (12 entries) covering the project's evolution
from initial paper analysis through Phase 3. Key findings are now documented in
[Research Basis](../docs/design/research-basis.md).

## Why archived?

These files served as source material for the official documentation. They are
preserved here for provenance — the raw thinking behind the design decisions.
For current documentation, see `docs/`.
